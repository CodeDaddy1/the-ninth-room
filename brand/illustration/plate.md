# The illustration style contract

**Status: DRAFT for Caleb's signature, statement by statement.** Until the
header reads SIGNED, the illustration-director quotes it as handed and
Caleb judges the result on the Library desk.

The runner replaces every placeholder below (a token name in double
braces) with the hex the design system holds at run time (`brand/design-system/tokens/`, through
`pipeline/design_tokens.py`), so no colour is ever typed here or in a
prompt. A prompt that carries a hex is refused by `validate_round`.

## Plate (honesty: illustration)

Cyanotype scientific plate, in the manner of a nineteenth century
botanical print. Ground: deep Prussian blue, `{{navy_900}}`. Subject and
detail: chalk white, `{{chalk}}`, with the mid-tones in pale blue,
`{{slate_300}}`, drawn as fine engraved line, never painted. Soft paper
grain over the whole plate; a deckled paper border on all four sides.
No text, no labels, no watermark, no yellow, no cyan: the plate is the
illustration, the kit puts words on it. One subject, centred or on a
third; the leaf, the bone, the machine fills the frame the way a specimen
fills a plate. Motion, when there is motion: the subject moves and the
plate does not; a slow push-in is allowed and the border may leave the
frame; the colours never shift.

## Reconstruction (honesty: reconstruction)

Photoreal, and marked. The kit eyebrow RECONSTRUCTION sits over it for
its whole length, and the description carries the disclosure. Light and
lens as if filmed on the visit's own camera: natural light, a long lens,
handheld stillness, no cinematic grade. The grade is the channel's: cool
shadows toward `{{navy_900}}`, highlights toward `{{chalk}}`, skin and
stone left true. Never the real place as it stands today; a
reconstruction shows a time or a thing the visit could not film, and the
research facts on the round say which. No people unless the round's Soul
step puts the host in it, and never the family. No text in the image.

## What both refuse

The place itself. A real person other than the host, and the host outside
a reconstruction or a thumbnail. Any word or number in the picture. A
hex colour typed into a prompt. The word insert.
