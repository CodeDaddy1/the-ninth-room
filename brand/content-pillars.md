# Content Pillars

Every piece of content belongs to one of these pillars. Pillars keep the feed
coherent, make planning easier, and train the audience on what to expect. If an
idea doesn't fit a pillar, flag it — don't force it.

Aim for a rotation so the feed never feels one-note. Suggested mix is in
`workflows/posting-cadence.md`.

---

### 1. Hidden in Plain Sight
Everyday objects, words, and habits with a surprising backstory. *The aglet on
your shoelace, why keyboards are QWERTY, what the dot on the "i" is called.*
- **Why it works:** instant relatability — "I see this every day and never knew."
- **Best formats:** Reels, carousels.

### 2. Unsolved & Unexplained
Genuine open questions, mysteries, and things science hasn't cracked.
*The Voynich manuscript, why we yawn, the Wow! signal.*
- **Why it works:** the curiosity gap stays open honestly — the payoff is "no
  one knows, and here's why that's fascinating."
- **Best formats:** YouTube deep-dives, Reels.
- **Care:** label clearly as unsolved; never imply a fake answer.

### 3. How It Really Works
Satisfying reveals of mechanisms and processes. *How a zipper actually closes,
how GPS knows where you are, how cheese gets its holes.*
- **Why it works:** delivers a clean, complete payoff every time.
- **Best formats:** Shorts, long-form, carousels.

### 4. Lost & Forgotten
History, places, people, and inventions that time skipped over. *The library of
Alexandria's real fate, forgotten everyday inventions, abandoned megaprojects.*
- **Why it works:** narrative + wonder, great for long-form.
- **Best formats:** YouTube long-form, carousels.

### 5. Scales of Wonder
The very small and the very large — micro worlds and cosmic vastness. *Tardigrades,
the size of the observable universe, what's inside a single drop of pond water.*
- **Why it works:** awe is the most shareable emotion.
- **Best formats:** Reels (visual-led), Shorts.

### 6. The Human Strange
Psychology, behavior, and the quirks of being a person. *Why songs get stuck in
your head, the science of déjà vu, why we find babies cute.*
- **Why it works:** maximally relatable — it's about *them*.
- **Best formats:** Reels, carousels, long-form.

---

## Pillar discipline

- Tag every piece of content with its pillar in the planning doc.
- Track which pillars over- and under-perform per platform (the
  performance-analyst owns this) and rebalance the rotation accordingly.
- Pillars can evolve — revisit this list quarterly once there's real data.
