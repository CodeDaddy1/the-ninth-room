# Content Pillars — The Ninth Room

Every episode belongs to one pillar. Pillars keep the channel coherent, make
planning easy, and train the audience on what to expect. If an idea doesn't
fit a pillar, flag it — don't force it.

The unit is **a room**, not a topic. A room is whatever a museum treats as one
space: a hall, a wing, a vault, a greenhouse. One room, one episode, one week.

---

### 1. The Big Dead Things
Fossils, bones, skeletons, and the animals behind them. *Eremotherium the
giant ground sloth, the whale in the atrium, a mosasaur nobody looks up at.*
- **Why it works:** scale is the most immediate wonder there is. A number in
  feet does more than a paragraph.
- **Cards that fit:** stat, quiz, verdict, scale.

### 2. Small and Alive
Insects, butterflies, reptiles, the greenhouse, anything breathing.
*The Cockrell Butterfly Center, a cockroach nobody will hold, leafcutter ants.*
- **Why it works:** it's the only pillar where something can happen *to* the
  family on camera. A butterfly picking one person is a whole beat.
- **Cards that fit:** vote, countdown, prediction, spot it.

### 3. Rocks That Are Worth More Than Your House
Gems, minerals, meteorites, the vault. *A meteorite older than the planet, the
gem vault, fluorescence rooms.*
- **Why it works:** the value gap is comic and the science is genuinely
  strange. Both land in the same sentence.
- **Cards that fit:** stat, this-or-that, rank, poll.

### 4. Empires and the Dead
Human history, artefacts, tombs, and the things civilisations left behind.
*The Hall of Ancient Egypt, King Tut, a shrine inside a shrine.*
- **Why it works:** narrative pillar. Best long-form retention, strongest
  chapter structure.
- **Cards that fit:** true or false, quiz, rank, takeaway.

### 5. How This Building Works
The museum itself — the mechanisms, the staff, the things on display that are
really instruments. *The Foucault pendulum, the planetarium, conservation
labs.*
- **Why it works:** delivers a clean, complete payoff every time, and reframes
  something the viewer has walked past for years.
- **Cards that fit:** callout, lower third, streak, countdown.

### 6. The Ninth Room
The pillar that is the channel's name: the thing that wasn't on the map. A
mislabelled case, a door marked staff only, a fact the docent volunteers only
if you ask.
- **Why it works:** it is the promise. It is also the reason to watch to the
  end, because that's where it lands.
- **Care:** this pillar cannot be manufactured. If we didn't find one, we say
  so and the takeaway carries the episode instead. **A fake ninth room is the
  fastest way to lose the audience.**
- **Cards that fit:** callout, spot it, quote, takeaway.

---

## Pillar discipline

- Tag every episode with its pillar in `work/<slug>/edit_plan.json`.
- Rotate. Two consecutive Big Dead Things episodes make the channel feel like
  a fossil channel; the museum is more interesting than that.
- Every episode carries **at least one** engagement card and **exactly one**
  takeaway. See `engagement-playbook.md`.
- Revisit this list once there is real per-pillar retention data.
