# Visual Identity

The visual system feels like the brand: curious, clean, and a little wondrous.
It must be instantly recognizable in a crowded feed and legible at thumbnail
size on a phone.

> **Direction locked: "Wonder."** A magnifying glass observing a constellation
> — the lens is the curation, the stars are the wonder, the brightest star in
> amber is the find. Source files in `brand/logo-wonder-magnifier.svg`
> (production exports to follow).

## Logo & wordmark
- **Concept:** magnifier with a constellation magnified inside the lens; faint
  wider starfield hints at the broader sky beyond.
- **Wordmark:** "CURATED" in high-contrast serif caps with wide letterspacing,
  cream; "CURIOSITIES" below in letterspaced sans caps, amber. Stacked beneath
  the icon for the primary lockup.
- **Submark / icon-only:** the magnifier+constellation mark used standalone for
  profile pics, end cards, and merch.
- **Watermark (small-scale):** simplified to the magnifier with a *single*
  amber star inside the lens. Constellation chains do not render at watermark
  size and become noise.

## Color palette
A four-color system: deep navy base, amber as the single bold accent, cream
for type and light, slate for muted/secondary.

- **Midnight Navy `#0E1B2C`** — primary background, the night-sky base.
- **Amber `#E8A33D`** — the accent: the observed star, key hook words, the
  one-color-that-pops. Use sparingly for emphasis.
- **Cream `#F4EFE6`** — primary text and the lens itself.
- **Slate `#6B7C93`** — secondary text, captions, muted UI.

Panel/darker variant for nested surfaces (e.g. inside banners): `#0A1422`.
Hairline rule: `#243449`.

Guidance: navy + amber is the brand moment; cream + slate carry the type. A
darker base with one bold accent reads "premium curiosity," not loud clickbait.

## Typography
- **Display / hook font:** high-contrast serif — Didot, Bodoni MT, or Playfair
  Display feel. Used for the wordmark, video titles, and on-screen hooks. Set
  in caps with generous letterspacing on the wordmark. *Specific webfont to
  lock at production export.*
- **Body / UI font:** a clean humanist or geometric sans for captions,
  carousels, and on-screen body text. *To lock at production.*
- Rule: hook text must be readable in under a second at thumbnail size.

## Thumbnail & cover system (the most important visual asset)
A consistent thumbnail formula trains recognition and lifts click-through.
- **One focal subject**, large and clear.
- **3–5 words max** of high-contrast text — the curiosity gap, not the answer.
- Consistent placement of text and logo so the brand is recognizable as a row.
- Faces/expressions or a single striking object outperform busy scenes.
- Avoid clutter; negative space is your friend.

## Reels / Shorts on-screen text
- Big, bold, center-safe (away from platform UI overlays).
- Captions on by default — most watch muted.
- Keep the brand accent color for emphasis words.

## Carousels
- Slide 1 = the hook (works as a standalone thumbnail).
- Consistent template: title bar, body, brand mark in the same spot.
- Last slide = takeaway + soft CTA (follow / save / "more on this").
