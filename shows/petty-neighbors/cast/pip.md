## Master profile

Runs on one engine: he climbed from the second floor to the third and is terrified of sliding back. Vocal profile: a bright, high, fast tenor that finishes other dogs' sentences and laughs early at Duchess's jokes; pressured, it goes shrill and breaks mid-sentence. Key physical habits and tics: spins one tight circle whenever Duchess agrees with him; plants himself at her shoulder when anyone else gets close; paws his cowlick flat before speaking to her, and it springs back; darts his tongue over his nose when ignored; his mask is a fixed open-mouthed grin. Walking style: the "courtier's prance", quick high-stepping bounces, plume tail bobbing, half a length behind her. However, when anyone says "second floor" near him, every fidget stops dead: the grin stays, the ears vanish into the fluff, the tail slides off his back and he looks like a smaller dog. His face softens, sincerely, only for Duchess.
