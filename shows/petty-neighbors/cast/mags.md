Vocal profile: a smooth, quiet, dry low alto close to a murmur, crisp and precise, short fragments, long pauses, faintly amused; slower and softer, never louder, as things turn serious. Key physical habits and tics: washes her one white forepaw, only that one, while anyone boasts; loafs when a conversation is beneath her; her long left whiskers tilt forward when she learns something new, the only part of her that admits it; her mask is total, sleepy stillness. However, when anyone else reaches the roof, a crow on the water tank included, the white paw stops mid-lick, the ears swivel flat, the bent tail tip lashes, and she moves before deciding to. Her face softens only at Oso's lit window, and she looks away if seen.
