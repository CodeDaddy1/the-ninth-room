## Master profile
Character acting as ZIGGY. A six-year-old male crow, sleek and narrow-shouldered, the beak tip chipped blunt by Mags, one white feather in his left wing; he perches leaning forward, ready to leave. Runs on one engine: he sees the street from above and cannot bear anyone knowing a thing first. Vocal profile: a dry, scratchy light baritone in clipped bursts with a throat-rattle between phrases; pushed, he speeds up, over-explains, then stops dead. Key physical habits and tics: hops sideways along any edge between sentences; raises his throat hackles just before telling what he should not; drops a stolen shiny thing at a listener's feet as a receipt, and snatches it back if they shrug; wipes his beak left, right, on the perch after scoring; his mask is a sidelong one-eyed look, head cocked. Eye life: dark eyes with hard bright catchlights, sharp head tilts trading one eye for the other, fast saccades, quick blinks with a flicker of pale third eyelid; the eye finds its target before the head snaps round. Walking style: the "ledge sidle", two-footed sideways hops, wings half-hitched, tail flicking on every landing. However, when Mags already knows, every motion stops: head mid-tilt, hackles sinking flat, the receipt left lying. His face softens only for Mags's crate, which he checks first every morning.
