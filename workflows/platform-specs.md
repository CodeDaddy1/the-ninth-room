# Platform Specs

A quick cheat sheet so every piece ships in the right shape. Platform specs
drift over time — re-verify current limits with each platform's official help
docs periodically; treat the numbers below as a working baseline.

## Instagram

### Reels
- **Aspect:** 9:16 vertical (1080×1920)
- **Length:** keep most under 30s; up to 90s when the payoff earns it
- **Cover:** custom cover frame; first frame should still hook on autoplay
- **Captions:** burn in on-screen captions (most watch muted)
- **Caption text:** hook in the first line (before the "...more" cutoff)
- **Hashtags:** a focused set of relevant tags; quality over stuffing

### Carousels
- **Aspect:** 4:5 (1080×1350) recommended for feed real estate
- **Slides:** up to 10; aim 6–8
- **Slide 1:** doubles as the hook + thumbnail — make it stop the scroll
- **Last slide:** takeaway + soft CTA (save / follow / "more on YouTube")

### Stories
- **Aspect:** 9:16
- **Use:** polls, quizzes, teasers, reposting Reels, link-outs to YouTube

## YouTube

### Long-form
- **Aspect:** 16:9 (1920×1080+)
- **Length:** 6–12 min sweet spot for our deep-dives
- **Title:** curiosity gap, legible, no answer-spoiling
- **Thumbnail:** 1280×720, one focal subject, ≤5 words, readable tiny
- **First 30s:** restate the hook and promise the payoff — retention is decided
  here
- **Description:** summary + sources/citations + timestamps + links
- **End screen / pinned comment:** point to the next rabbit hole

### Shorts
- **Aspect:** 9:16 (1080×1920)
- **Length:** under 60s
- **Use:** repurpose Reels; tease long-form; standalone quick payoffs
- **Loop:** design the ending to invite a rewatch when it fits

## Universal rules

- **Safe zones:** keep key text away from platform UI overlays (bottom caption
  bar, right-side action buttons).
- **Sound-off first:** assume muted autoplay; carry the message visually.
- **One idea per piece:** especially short formats.
- **Sources:** keep citations in the YouTube description and ready to drop in
  IG comments if asked.
