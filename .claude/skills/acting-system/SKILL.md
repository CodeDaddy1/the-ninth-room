# The Acting System for Petty Neighbors

Read `doctrine.md` in full first. It is Caleb's pasted system, kept near verbatim. This file says
how it applies to a cast of animated animals. Where they disagree, this file wins.

## Who uses which part

| Agent | Uses |
|---|---|
| casting-director | sections 6, 7 and 9: one master profile and one voice prompt per character |
| skit-writer | sections 2 to 5 and 11: objective, obstacle, tactics, beats, subtext, status per scene |
| shot-director | sections 8 and 10: the per-shot scene adaptation, states not transitions, and the voice prompt pasted into AUDIO |
| everyone | sections 12 to 14 as the review bar; nothing ships under a 4 on the scale |

## Animals, not people

The doctrine is written for human actors. Translate every rule into the animal's body; never drop
one.

- **Center of gravity** is head carriage and haunches: a head held high with the chest out is high
  status; head below the shoulders, haunches tucked, is low.
- **The honest tells** are ears, tail and whiskers. Every tic and every "However, when..." crack
  names which of them moves: ears pinned flat, a tail that stops mid-sweep, whiskers pulled back.
- **Business** is done with paws, mouth and nose: batting a bottle cap, nosing a food bowl, licking
  a paw to look unbothered. The interrupted-action rule applies: the paw that stops mid-lick.
- **Gaits are four-legged.** Name them for a quadruped ("the lobby strut", "the alley prowl") and
  unpack weight, step order and what the head and tail do.
- **Eye life is mandatory and harder.** Painterly 3D eyes are large and read every flaw: write
  saccades, blink quality, catchlights and eyes-lead-the-head for every animal in frame.
- **Mugging is the biggest risk.** Big cartoon faces invite indication (atlas row 1). The comedy is
  played dead serious; the face thinks, it does not perform.
- **No wardrobe in the profile.** A collar, bandana or tag belongs in the cast file's look anchors.

## Voice prompts

One per character, written once by casting-director, approved by Caleb, then pasted verbatim into
every shot where that character speaks. Describe attitude, age, timbre, pace and how it shifts under
pressure. **No ethnic accent or dialect for any character.** Street credibility is attitude and
wear (gravel, economy, long pauses), never a borrowed accent.

## Where it lives

Master profiles and voice prompts live in `shows/petty-neighbors/cast/<id>.md`, under the headings
`## Master profile` and `## Voice prompt`. An agent never edits a profile marked `status: approved`
without Caleb's word in the session.
